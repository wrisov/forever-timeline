Timeline of Us

A growing memory timeline for everything we share, one photo and one moment at a time.

How to keep adding memories

  1. Drop your new photo files into the photos folder.
  2. Open timeline-data.js and add a new item with the date, title, description, and image path.
  3. Save the files and upload the whole project to your hosting platform.
  4. Any new item you add will stay in the site because it is no longer embedded directly into the HTML file.
